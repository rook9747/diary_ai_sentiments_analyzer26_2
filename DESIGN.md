## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an empathic, intimate, and modern sanctuary for emotional reflection. It pairs the structured intentionality of Google Material Design 3 (M3) with a warm, restorative sensibility tailored for personal journaling and AI-driven introspection.

### Design Principles
- **Compassionate Intelligence**: AI features feel like a mindful guide rather than a clinical algorithm. Visual surfaces lean warm and soft, avoiding stark cold whites or sterile technical motifs.
- **Expressive Clarity**: High typographic legibility with dynamic M3 color-role relationships that adapt smoothly to emotional nuances—from deep contemplation to vibrant joy.
- **Organic Softness**: Generous radii, pill-shaped interactions, and fluid motion establish an approachable, tactile environment that invites daily expression without intimidation.

## Colors

The palette balances introspective depth with radiant warmth, mapped to standard Material Design 3 functional roles.

### Tonal Foundations
- **Primary (`#6356E5` - Luminous Iris)**: Represents cognitive insight, thoughtful AI synthesis, and deep contemplation. Used for primary floating action buttons (FABs), key interactive triggers, and prominent active states.
- **Secondary (`#F48B6C` - Soft Coral / Warm Peach)**: Provides welcoming, human warmth. Governs supportive cards, secondary actions, and reflective daily prompts.
- **Tertiary (`#489F87` - Calming Sage)**: Serves as an emotional stabilizer for balance metrics, positive sentiment badges, and meditative states.
- **Neutral (`#7A757E` - Warm Slate)**: Ensures all surface tones skew slightly warm rather than neutral gray, grounding the reading and writing experience.

### Container & Surface Tiers
- **Surface**: `#FFFBF8` (Warm Alabaster)
- **Surface Container Lowest**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Container Low**: `#F9F3EE`
- **Surface Container**: `#F3EDE7`
- **Surface Container High**: `#EDE7E1`
- **Surface Container Highest**: `#E7E1DB`
- **On Surface**: `#1E1B1E`
- **On Surface Variant**: `#4B454D`
- **Outline**: `#7D757F`
- **Outline Variant**: `#CFC4CE`

### Emotion Indicator Accents
Emotion badges utilize vibrant, high-luminance tints mapped over soft container backgrounds:
- **Joy / Radiance**: Container `#FFE8B6`, Foreground `#755900`
- **Sadness / Release**: Container `#DDE5FF`, Foreground `#29437E`
- **Anger / Tension**: Container `#FFDAD7`, Foreground `#93000E`
- **Anxiety / Overwhelm**: Container `#F4DDFF`, Foreground `#6E3987`
- **Peace / Groundedness**: Container `#D0EADB`, Foreground `#1E4E3A`

## Typography

The typography pairing respects Material Design 3 type scales while optimizing for Korean (Hangul) and Latin multilingual legibility.

- **Primary Heading & Interface Labels (`Roboto Flex`)**: Provides modern geometric balance, optical clarity, and structured rhythm across navigation bars, modal headers, and emotional metric cards.
- **Reading & Longform Composition (`Noto Sans`)**: Provides optimized glyph legibility across complex multi-lingual longform diary prose, journaling textareas, and AI analytical feedback.

### Usage Standards
- Longform journal entries must default to `body-lg` with open line spacing (`lineHeight: 24px`) to reduce eye fatigue during prolonged reflection.
- AI commentary uses `body-md` set against `surface-container-low` to maintain clear visual separation from personal diary entries.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to an 8dp/8px base spacing grid consistent with Material Design 3 guidelines.

### Form Factors & Grid Columns
- **Mobile (0 - 599px)**: 4 fluid columns, `margin: 1rem` (16px), `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Single-column stack for journal entries and reflective timeline streams.
- **Tablet (600 - 839px)**: 8 fluid columns, `margin-tablet: 1.5rem` (24px), `gutter-tablet: 1.5rem` (24px). Split layout support for weekly emotional trends and daily entry previews.
- **Desktop (840px+)**: 12 fluid columns, constrained to a maximum content container of `1200px`, `margin-desktop: 2rem` (32px), `gutter-desktop: 1.5rem` (24px). Dual-pane configuration featuring calendar/mood index alongside the active writing canvas.

### Padding and Spacing Rhythms
- Card interior padding uses `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for mobile.
- Section gaps in dashboard flows scale using `space-xl` (32px) and `space-xxl` (48px) to reinforce an unhurried, airy cognitive environment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages M3 tonal elevation paired with low-contrast, softly tinted drop shadows. Color tinting overrides purely gray drop shadows, keeping depth tactile and warm.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat)**: `surface` base `#FFFBF8`. No shadow. Used for page backgrounds, non-interactive panels, and inline dividers.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Surfaces)**: `surface-container-low` tinted with soft coral/indigo ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0px 1px 3px 1px rgba(99, 86, 229, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px 0px rgba(30, 27, 30, 0.06)`. Used for timeline entries, mood cards, and list items.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Segmented Controls)**: `surface-container`: `box-shadow: 0px 2px 6px 2px rgba(99, 86, 229, 0.06), 0px 1px 2px 0px rgba(30, 27, 30, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (FAB & Navigation Drawers)**: `surface-container-high`: `box-shadow: 0px 4px 8px 3px rgba(99, 86, 229, 0.08), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(30, 27, 30, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Dialogs & AI Insight Overlays)**: `surface-container-highest`: `box-shadow: 0px 6px 10px 4px rgba(99, 86, 229, 0.10), 0px 2px 3px 0px rgba(30, 27, 30, 0.14)`.

### Stroke Hierarchy
Borders use `outline-variant` (`#CFC4CE`) at 1px width strictly for un-elevated components (such as text input fields and outlined action chips), ensuring content containers rely predominantly on surface tone shifts rather than hard outlines.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates M3 expressive geometry, prioritizing large, friendly curves to communicate comfort and warmth.

### Shape Roles
- **Corner Small (`8px` / `0.5rem`)**: Tooltips, menu items, internal segment tags.
- **Corner Medium (`12px` / `0.75rem`)**: Text input fields, emotional selector drop-downs, date pickers.
- **Corner Large (`16px` / `1rem`)**: Default cards, diary preview tiles, bottom sheets, search bars.
- **Corner Extra-Large (`28px` / `1.75rem`)**: AI conversational dialogue panels, weekly reflection summary cards, large modal dialogs.
- **Corner Full (Pill / `9999px`)**: Primary FABs, filter chips, action buttons, sentiment indicator tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Filled Button (Primary Action)**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `40px`, background `primary` (`#6356E5`), label `label-lg` in `#FFFFFF`. Hover state applies an 8% white overlay with Level 1 elevation.
- **Tonal Button (Secondary Action)**: Pill-shaped, background `surface-container-high`, label `label-lg` in `primary`. Used for secondary actions like "Save as Draft" or "Skip AI Analysis".
- **Outlined Button**: 1px border using `outline`, text `primary`, transparent background. On hover, fills with 8% opacity of `primary`.

### Floating Action Button (FAB)
- **Standard & Extended FAB**: Large rounded shape (`shape-corner-large`, 16px radius), background `secondary` (`#F48B6C`) with on-secondary `#FFFFFF` content. Hover transitions to Level 4 elevation. Extended FAB contains a 24px icon paired with `label-lg` for "Write Entry".

### Chips & Emotion Badges
- **Assist / Filter Chips**: Pill-shaped, height `32px`, border 1px `outline-variant`. Active filter fills with `secondary-container` (`#FFE9E2`) and text `on-secondary-container` (`#3B0900`).
- **Emotion Badges**: Pill-shaped, padding `4px 12px`, typography `label-sm`. Dynamically color-coded using the contextual emotion palette tiers (e.g., Joy: `#FFE8B6` surface with `#755900` text).

### Cards
- **Elevated Card**: Corner Large (16px), background `surface-container-low`, Level 1 elevation. Hover transitions elevation to Level 2.
- **Outlined Card**: Corner Large (16px), background `surface`, 1px border `outline-variant`. Used for nested prompt cards and daily writing prompts.
- **AI Summary Card**: Corner Extra-Large (28px), background linear gradient from `surface-container-low` to `#F7F2FD` (primary-tinted surface container), subtle 1px border of `primary` at 15% opacity.

### Input Fields
- **Diary Writing Area (Filled Text Field)**: Corner Large (top corners 16px, flat bottom with indicator line, or fully rounded 16px without container boundary). Background `surface-container-lowest`. Typography `body-lg`. Placeholder in `on-surface-variant` at 60% opacity.
- **Prompts & Search Fields**: Fully pill-shaped search bar, height `48px`, background `surface-container-high`, leading icon in `on-surface-variant`.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Checkbox adopts 4px rounded radius; Radio adopts pure circular shape. Active state filled with `primary` (`#6356E5`), inactive state bordered by `outline`. Ripple feedback effect uses soft lavender highlight.

### Lists
- Multi-line timeline entries feature `space-md` inner padding, separated by subtle full-width spacers or 1px `surface-container-highest` dividers. Leading avatar contains emotional mood badge glyph; trailing element displays timestamp formatted with `label-sm`.
